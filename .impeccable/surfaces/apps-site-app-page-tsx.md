# Surface brief: Helium public entrance and workspace

## Scope and mode
- `/` landing: Persuade. `/workspace/**`: Operate, same world.
- Audience: hackathon judges and teams evaluating Helium, equal weight.
- Job: understand in one viewport that Helium keeps a team's decisions as dated, sourced layers; then open the live workspace.
- Proof: the real Orbit corpus chain (UPI flow 24 Sep, then 25 Sep, then 28 Sep; Razorpay, then PayU), real quotes, real Cypher, measured bench numbers. Synthetic corpus is labelled as a sample.
- Constraints: nothing may resemble Minutes (localhost:3001). Reduced motion, keyboard, non-WebGL fallback.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Team memory is an ice core. Every day of talk is a layer, nothing melts, and drilling to a depth reads what the team believed on that date. Refuses the category default of a centered SaaS hero, card grid and node-link hairball.
OWN-WORLD: Dark-field core lab. Polar-night navy ground (#0b111c), frost white type, translucent ice with cloudy bands and trapped bubbles (detail from the real GISP2 core), ice cyan for live data, one helium peach (#ffae8a, the glow of a helium discharge tube) reserved for what is in force and the primary action, rose flag for needs review, struck ash for superseded, cool white light-table sheets for evidence. Archivo across widths (expanded display, semi-condensed labels), Spectral italic only for human words, JetBrains Mono only for Cypher and IDs. State is a mark: struck line, tape band, folded flag, pencil outline. (Revised 16 Oct 2026: the first teal ground and helium-yellow accent were rejected by the user.)
STORY: Visitor sees a real core whose layers are Orbit's meetings and emails, drills from 16 Oct down to 7 Sep watching one date change three times, scrubs any day to read the belief, checks the receipts and Cypher, sees agents wake on a change, then opens the workspace.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left two-thirds: headline "Every decision leaves a layer." at display scale, one sentence, primary action "Open the workspace" in helium peach, secondary "Drill into September". Right third: the 3D core, tilted, layers tagged with dates. Far-left depth rail reading 0.0 m / 16 Oct.
FORM: Ice core log, rank 1 of the grounded list, chosen over the roll on the user's instruction "choose what you like"; seed 43c41f6d. Raises: marks not hues (cutting bench rail); band thickness encodes elapsed days (coiled earth tower); one depth control drives the whole belief view (variable font specimen); every bubble is a real episode (hand-processed film); dates as navigation cells on the depth rail (theatre poster); every fact carries its ID ruled beneath (toy catalogue).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
